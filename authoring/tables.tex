\subsection*{Markdown}
\label{markdown}

Tables can use the standard Github-Flavored Markdown pipe syntax. Column alignment is controlled by colons in the separator row: left (default), right (\texttt{-{}-{}-:}), or center (\texttt{:-{}-{}-:}).

\medskip
\begin{tblr}{colspec={Q[l]Q[r]X[l]}, hline{1,2,Z}={solid}}
Name & Score & Grade \\
Alice & 95 & A \\
Bob & 87 & B \\
Carol & 92 & A \\
\end{tblr}
\medskip

\subsubsection*{Captions and cross-references}
\label{captions-and-cross-references}

Wrap a table in a fenced div with a \texttt{\#tbl-} id to add a caption and make it cross-referenceable. The last paragraph in the div becomes the caption:




\begin{srccode}
\begin{Verbatim}[commandchars=\\\{\}]
\textcolor{hl1}{::: \{\#tbl-grades\}
}
\textcolor{hl1}{| Name  | Score | Grade |
}
\textcolor{hl1}{|-------|------:|:-----:|
}
\textcolor{hl1}{| Alice |    95 | A     |
}
\textcolor{hl1}{| Bob   |    87 | B     |
}
\textcolor{hl1}{| Carol |    92 | A     |
}

\textcolor{hl1}{Student grades for Fall 2026.
}
\textcolor{hl1}{:::}
\end{Verbatim}
\end{srccode}

\begin{table}
\centering
\caption{Student grades for Fall 2026.}
\label{tbl-grades}
\medskip
\begin{tblr}{colspec={Q[l]Q[r]X[l]}, hline{1,2,Z}={solid}}
Name & Score & Grade \\
Alice & 95 & A \\
Bob & 87 & B \\
Carol & 92 & A \\
\end{tblr}
\medskip


\end{table}

Reference tables with \texttt{Table \ref{tbl-label}}: \hyperref[tbl-grades]{Table 1} shows the full class results. Suppress the type prefix with \texttt{\ref{tbl-label}}: \hyperref[tbl-grades]{1}.

\subsubsection*{Caption location}
\label{caption-location}

By default, captions appear above the table. Use \texttt{tbl-cap-location} to place them below:



\begin{table}
\centering
\caption{This caption appears below the table.}
\label{tbl-bottom}
\medskip
\begin{tblr}{colspec={X[l]X[l]}, hline{1,2,Z}={solid}}
x & y \\
1 & 1 \\
2 & 4 \\
3 & 9 \\
\end{tblr}
\medskip


\end{table}

\subsection*{R}
\label{r}

The \href{https://vincentarelbundock.github.io/tinytable/}{tinytable} R package generates format-aware tables that work automatically in \emph{Calepin}. Use a \texttt{tbl-} label on the chunk and \texttt{tbl-cap} for the caption. The \texttt{style\_tt()} function applies cell-level formatting, and \texttt{group\_tt()} adds spanning headers.




\begin{srccode}
\begin{Verbatim}[commandchars=\\\{\}]
\textcolor{hl1}{```\{r, tbl}\textcolor{hl8}{-}\textcolor{hl1}{cars\}}
\textcolor{hl10}{\#| tbl-cap = Motor Trend car data
}
\textcolor{hl10}{\#| echo = fenced
}
\textcolor{hl3}{library}\textcolor{hl1}{(tinytable)}
\textcolor{hl3}{tt}\textcolor{hl1}{(mtcars[}\textcolor{hl3}{1}\textcolor{hl8}{:}\textcolor{hl3}{5}\textcolor{hl1}{, }\textcolor{hl3}{1}\textcolor{hl8}{:}\textcolor{hl3}{4}\textcolor{hl1}{]) }\textcolor{hl8}{|>}
\textcolor{hl1}{  }\textcolor{hl3}{style\_tt}\textcolor{hl1}{(}\textcolor{hl3}{2}\textcolor{hl8}{:}\textcolor{hl3}{3}\textcolor{hl1}{, }\textcolor{hl3}{background}\textcolor{hl1}{ }\textcolor{hl8}{=}\textcolor{hl1}{ }\textcolor{hl2}{"\#5E81AC"}\textcolor{hl1}{, }\textcolor{hl3}{color}\textcolor{hl1}{ }\textcolor{hl8}{=}\textcolor{hl1}{ }\textcolor{hl2}{"white"}\textcolor{hl1}{) }\textcolor{hl8}{|>}
\textcolor{hl1}{  }\textcolor{hl3}{group\_tt}\textcolor{hl1}{(}\textcolor{hl3}{j}\textcolor{hl1}{ }\textcolor{hl8}{=}\textcolor{hl1}{ }\textcolor{hl8}{list}\textcolor{hl1}{(}\textcolor{hl2}{"Group"}\textcolor{hl1}{ }\textcolor{hl8}{=}\textcolor{hl1}{ }\textcolor{hl3}{3}\textcolor{hl8}{:}\textcolor{hl3}{4}\textcolor{hl1}{))}
\textcolor{hl1}{```}
\end{Verbatim}
\end{srccode}
