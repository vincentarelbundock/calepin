
  cfg.* (user-authored values) -- from front matter, attributes, labels, structured data

  Chunk options and div attributes are copied as-is to cfg.* and available
  in templates directly (e.g., cfg.fig_pos, cfg.fig_scap, cfg.tbl_pos).

  Literal braces in LaTeX templates: use {{ '{' }} and {{ '}' }}

  Other

  - Local variables ({% set %}): title (callouts), italic (theorem),
    listing_type (website/listing)
  - Filters: default(), length, replace(), safe, title
  - Functions: link() (website templates, URL resolution)
  - Loop variables: item, child, node, crumb, t
    Sub-properties: .active, .children, .date, .description, .file, .flag,
    .href, .icon, .image, .image_dark, .height, .lang, .name, .text,
    .title, .url, .width
  - Loop helpers: loop.first, loop.last, loop.depth, loop.depth0, loop.index
